Modular services

We custom build and/or improve only what you need.

Launch & Acquisition

Mass Tort Infrastructure Build-Out

Ongoing selection and management of highly vetted vendors: call centers, intake flows, landing pages, questionnaire forms, integrations, CRM systems, medical records retrieval and review vendors, and case workup vendors for filing and litigation.

Marketing & Ad Spend Management

Multi-channel campaign creation and management (ads, creatives, digital and organic advertising, landing pages); cost-per-acquisition analysis; market saturation and risk assessments; recommendations on whether to scale or pause campaigns based on cost, performance, and quality.

Referral Partners

Access to additional litigations and introductions to potential referral partners.

Conversion & Validation

Flow Optimization

Build processes and automation journeys that move high quality leads to the next step and yield end-to-end improvement of the mass tort flow: advertising, intake, retention, automated questionnaires, validation, engagement, lead tiering, and follow-up.

Lead & Case Validation

Implement processes that validate leads, then advance validated cases toward filing readiness.

Special Projects

Plaintiff fact sheet collection and review; secondary client questionnaires; re-engagement with unresponsive clients; development of time-sensitive campaigns that need a dedicated, purpose-built workflow.

Value & Capital

Case Value & Underwriting

Underwriting and ongoing ROI calculations per tort for better decision making.

Tort & Portfolio Strategy

Strategic input on timing to invest, cost, and expected value across the portfolio.

Litigation Funding Readiness

Funding strategies; access to litigation funding capital; funder due diligence and proposal preparation; ROI calculations that clarify the value of claims.

Reporting & Dashboards

Trend analysis and streamlined reports that track status, quality, ROI, and lead and client volume.
